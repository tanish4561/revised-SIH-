Game Mechanics/ Flow:-

1) There will two roles: a) Rescuer b) Chemical Expert
   a) Rescuer Goals:-
     i) Get the affected personnel to the cold zone 
     ii) Decontaminate/ treat these individuals
   b) Chemical experts:-
     i) Locate the cold zone and Detect the type of gas
     ii) Deploy the containment devices to prevent further spread of the gas

2)Players will be assigned their roles after they enter the game

3)PLayers will be spawned 100 to 200 meters away from the leakage site/factory

4)The truck would contain the multigas detector to detect the lel% and O2 level. Players are required to measure the O2 levels and with regards to this choose an appropriate PPE suit
   i) Suit A: hazmat without SCBA
   ii) Suit B: hazmat with SCBA

5)After finding out the lel and O2 reading the players are to finish the tasks assigned as per their role.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meterics for Evaluation which will be fed to the backend:-
A) For Rescuer
  1) no. of people successfully rescued
  2) no. of people decontaminated
  3) total time taken

B) For Chemical Guy
  1) Detect the correct type of the gas
  2) Correct placement/use of containment equipment
  3) total time taken

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dashboard:-
From the Dashboard (to the backend to the game)
The admin on the dashboard will click a create session button
- Then will have an option on the dashboard to select the number of players
- and select what roles the players will have
- then start the session
-- this will send the session id to the backend along with the number of players (and their userids) and roles

after entering this session id on the dashboard, they will obtain a invite link ingame to join the lobby

After the game is completed 
- The game sends a json to the backend with the metrics for evaluation
- which the backend works on and sends to the dashboard
